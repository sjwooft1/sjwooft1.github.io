Two-device timing

Finish Line Camera

Camera off

Start the race on your other device, then film the finish line here. Every runner who crosses the marked line gets a finish time against that shared clock — no bib reading involved, so a smudged number can never cost a time. Crossings are assigned in expected finish order; write the real bibs down and correct them below. Only the chest counts: the time stamps when the athlete's torso reaches the line — swinging arms, trailing feet and heads above the strip are ignored.

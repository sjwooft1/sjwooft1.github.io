Setup checklist
  • Camera 6–15 ft behind the line, chest height, facing oncoming runners
  • Drag the orange line exactly onto the finish line in view
  • Bibs should be front-facing and unobstructed
  • Even light — avoid hard shadows and glare across the chute
  • Do a test walk-through before the gun; adjust confidence if needed

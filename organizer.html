Two-device timing

Finish Line Camera

Camera off

Start the race on your other device (or let the start gun start it), then film the finish line here. Every runner who crosses the marked line gets a finish time against that shared clock — no bib reading involved, so a smudged number can never cost a time. The detector follows the whole runner through the band below and stamps the moment their centre meets the line; if they are briefly hidden behind somebody, the crossing is still recorded. Crossings are assigned in expected finish order — write the real bibs down and correct them below. On a phone the preview opens fullscreen on its own and keeps the screen awake, so the camera stays open from the gun to the last runner.

Scenes from the Course

Est. Thanksgiving 2025

Some Things Never Change

The same pines. The same dirt roads. The same old country store on the corner. The Brighton Oaks 5K is a run back in time — a Thanksgiving morning tradition for the whole town, from the gas pumps at the start to the porch at the finish.

Grab a friend, pin on a number, and become part of the story.

Run, Race, and Celebrate!

Join us for a fun and fast 5K through the wonderful scenery of Brighton Oaks, South Carolina! This first annual Thanksgiving Day run welcomes all — from seasoned runners to first-timers. Enjoy the course, community, and celebration. Registration closes November 22, but exceptions can be made — fill out our email form.

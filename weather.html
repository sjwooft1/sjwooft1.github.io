TV & remote

The TV window runs in kiosk mode: edge-to-edge, no bars, auto-hiding cursor, screen stays awake. If your browser asks, allow fullscreen (and multi-screen if offered) — then drag the window to the TV with Win+Shift+←/→.
Open that link on your phone to drive the station (next / pause / pick cameras / layout). Same Wi-Fi computer: the remote pairs automatically. Other devices: type the pairing code shown on the remote.
Relay lets the phone control the TV over the internet — commands and station config travel through a public MQTT broker, scoped to your pairing code. Turn it off for local-only control.
